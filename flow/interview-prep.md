# Interview prep

Input: an application whose `Outcome` is `Interview`, plus what the owner knows about the interview (date, format, who they will meet). Ask for whatever is missing. Output: `outreach/<slug>/prep.md`, which the owner reads before the call.

1. Reread `jd.md`, `contacts.md`, `messages.md` and the reply thread: what the company was told is what the owner must be ready to expand on.
2. Research, with the year in each search: what the company sells and to whom, news from the last six months, funding and size, the product named in the JD, and each interviewer's role and public work. Keep the source link beside every fact.
3. Write `prep.md`:
   - **The role in three lines**: what they need, in the JD's own words.
   - **Fit map**: each JD requirement → the hook or CV line that answers it → the number or result that proves it. A requirement with no answer is a **gap**: write the honest answer the owner will give.
   - **Stories**: three to five, each as situation, the owner's own action, result, taken from `brief.md` → "Hooks". Facts only from the CV and `brief.md`; a missing detail is a question for the owner.
   - **Likely questions**: ten, mixed (motivation, role-specific, behavioural, the gaps), each with answer notes of three lines at most.
   - **Their questions**: five the owner can ask, tied to something found in step 2.
   - **Logistics**: date, time zone, format, names and titles, salary position from `brief.md` → "Deal-breakers", notice period.
   - **Off limits**: the list from `brief.md`, as a reminder.
4. Offer a mock interview: you ask the likely questions one at a time and give one concrete improvement per answer.
5. Add a follow-up row dated the day after the interview: "thank-you note to <interviewer>", which the owner sends as a reply on the scheduling thread.

**Done when** `prep.md` has every section, each company fact carries its source, each claim about the owner traces to the CV or `brief.md`, and the owner has read it.
